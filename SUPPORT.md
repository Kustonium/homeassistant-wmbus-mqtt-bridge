# Support

[Polska wersja](SUPPORT_PL.md)

This add-on is maintained in spare time and is not a general support desk.

- **Best effort.** One person maintains it after hours. I answer when I can; there is no guaranteed response time.
- **Not a business.** I do not run a company and do not issue invoices. There is no paid support or service contract.
- **No warranty.** The add-on is provided under GPL-3.0, without warranty of any kind (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). Readings used for billing are your responsibility.
- If the add-on helps you, [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/Kustonium) is welcome but never required.

## Open an issue for

- a reproducible bug in the add-on or its WebUI,
- a meter that is received but decoded wrongly (include the telegram hex and what the meter's display shows),
- a regression between versions,
- a documentation mistake.

## Before opening an issue

- read the [README](README.md) and its troubleshooting section,
- include the add-on version, the receiver (board and radio), the relevant add-on log lines and, for a meter, the telegram hex.

General Home Assistant, MQTT broker or ESPHome questions belong to their own communities. The receiver side (ESP firmware) has its own project: [esphome-wmbus-bridge-rawonly](https://github.com/Kustonium/esphome-wmbus-bridge-rawonly).

How to contribute code or translations: [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
